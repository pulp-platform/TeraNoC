>>> COMPARISON WITH THE GVSOC MODEL, AND THE SURPRISE
      RTL   0.634 beats/cyc within an entry
      model 0.96                              -> the MODEL is ~1.5x FASTER per entry

  This REFUTES the prediction (~2.0) made before the run. The RTL does not win by making one
  burst's beats return quickly; per entry it is slower than the model.

  It reconciles with the core-side result (RTL 2.00 words/commit-cycle vs model 1.00) only through
  CONCURRENCY: RTL throughput comes from many slow entries overlapping, not from any single entry
  being fast. Per-entry return width and aggregate throughput are different axes, and the RTL wins
  on the second while losing on the first.

⚠️ SCOPE: remote, ENTRY-CAPTURED bursts only. Bypassed bursts have no entry (mshr_tag=0) and are
invisible here -- and they are 50.5% of this workload. Because a burst bypasses exactly when banks
are full, entry-captured bursts are the UNCONGESTED subset, so 0.634 is if anything optimistic.
Intra-group bursts never reach the MSHR at all.
